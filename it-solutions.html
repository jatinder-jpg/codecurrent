Networking

A reliable network is the base of every other system in the building. We design it for speed, coverage and security.

  • Structured cabling: Cat6 copper and fibre, racks and patch panels
  • LAN design with switches and routers, plus VLANs for different teams
  • Wi-Fi survey and access-point placement for full coverage
  • Firewalls, VPN and secure remote access
  • Server rooms, UPS and power backup planning
  • Network monitoring and annual maintenance plans
Ideal for: new offices, co-working spaces, showrooms, schools, clinics and multi-floor buildings.

Yes. We visit your location, discuss your needs and then send a detailed quote. There is no obligation.

Yes. Many clients combine networking, CCTV and access control in one project, which saves cost and cabling.
